Web Development Requirements Specification

Description 

The following requirements specification outlines the redesign of Claire Daverley’s author website, whose next book is set to release in the US in 2027, following her debut title *Talking at Night*. In preparation for this exciting milestone, a website upgrade in aesthetic, ease of purchase, and inclusion of interactive opportunities will encourage reader engagement. This site is for literary and contemporary romance readers to provide connection opportunities with the author and access to her books by creating an immersive, visually cohesive brand experience.

1. Project Overview

- Project Name: Redesign of Claire Daverley’s Author Website

- Project Vision Statement:

- For **readers of Claire Daverley**, who want a **deeper connection to the author and her work**, the author website is **a central hub for discovering her titles and following her creative presence online**. It will provide **access to her social media channels** (Substack, Instagram), **embedded media clips, a complete events page, and well-structured product pages for both of her titles**. 

- Unlike **comparative author sites** (Ex. Alice Winn or Gabrielle Zevin) my product will display a **more curated aesthetic tied to her book cover design** and provide more **prominent purchase pathways** for the author’s titles. 

- Project Goals:

- Uplift the aesthetic of Daverley’s site to align with the visual appeal of her book covers 

- Provide both fans and new readers of Claire Daverley an easy, straightforward way to purchase her books

- Guide site visitors to Daverley’s event page and the subsequent venue sites to purchase tickets or RSVP

- Showcase Daverley’s media appearances (not available on current site)

- Target Audience: 

- Women aged 18 – 35

- Readers of literary fiction and contemporary romance books 

- Fans of Claire Daverley’s publications (*Talking at Night, People in Love*) 

- Fans of comparative authors to Claire Daverley (Sally Rooney, Alice Winn, etc.)

3. Functional Requirements

3.1 Navigation Bar

- Available on every page 

- The following categories are listed and linked:

- Home

- Books (links to a main “Books” page, with the option to jump to her two title pages)

- Events

- About

- Contact

- Horizontal across the top of the page, and on mobile as a “hamburger” in the top left corner of each page

3.2 Website Footer

- Available on every page 

- Full list of linked headers available from the navigation bar / menu

- Links to both the author’s Substack and Instagram profiles 

3.3 Home Page

- Book covers of both titles prominent at the top of the page, each image linked to respective product page

- Upcoming events (2-3) listed, with link to “Event” page for more detail 

- Embedded area to subscribe to Substack newsletter (or button to subscribe via Substack if module is not available)

- Embedded links to recent media, including:

- Podcasts (module available on website to play)

- Taped interviews (module shows video thumbnail image and ability to play)

- Authored articles for digital publications (article thumbnail where available)

3.4 Product Pages (Books)

- Both titles (*Talking at Night *and *People in Love*) presented on the main page (cover images) with title below the image (linked to respective product pages)

- Each product’s page will include:

- Cover image

- Title, author, release date (and other relevant metadata)

- Tagline and title description

- Towards the top of the page, prominent buttons containing links to purchase title at multitude of retailer sites for both US and UK 

- US: B&N, Bookshop.org, Target, Amazon, etc. 

- UK: Waterstones, Blackwells, Amazon, etc.

- Reviews from publications (praise)

3.5 Author Bio / About Page

- Prominent full-color author photo 

- Detailed author biography 

- Buttons containing links to her social media pages (Instagram and Substack)

- Embedded Substack module (most recent or most popular article)

- Embedded Instagram module (recent posts on author’s feed)

- Embedded links to recent media (podcasts, taped interviews, authored articles for digital publications)

3.6 Events Page

- List of upcoming events in date order (closest to current date at the top of the page)

- For each event:

- Title of event

- Event location (store / theater and city)

- Guest name (if applicable, interview partner for example)

- Button linked to purchase tickets to the event or RSVP on the venue event site

- Photo of Claire Daverley at an in-person event on the left side of the page

3.7 Contact Page

- Names, titles, and relevant email addresses (linked) for the author’s publicity representation and literary agent 

- Buttons containing links to her social media pages (Instagram and Substack)

5. Design and User Interface Requirements

- Design style:

- Background: Colorful artwork reminiscent of her book covers

- See cover here: [https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/720831/talking-at-night-by-claire-daverley/](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/720831/talking-at-night-by-claire-daverley/) 

- Font: Sans serif font (title font on cover) throughout all website pages

- Buttons: White background, rectangular with rounded edges, black sans serif text

- Navigation bar: One of the colors from the background with white text for all categories except “Books” (bright color from background to guide visitors to purchase)

- Responsive design requirements:

- Scroll functionality across all pages (including mobile access)

- Site has distinct mobile view: 

- Hamburger navigation menu available in the top left of the site

- Single column layout for content with images sized appropriately 

- Tablet view will mirror mobile view 

- Key UI elements:

- Navigation bar / menu across all pages (*see design details above*)

- Prominent buttons for links to other pages, purchasing her titles, and subscribing to her social media platforms (*see design details above*)

- Embedded links to media will feature visuals, such as thumbnail images (where available)

- Images will be full color and linked to relevant pages where applicable, such as the book covers to the product pages

- Footer text will be white to contrast with the background, present on every page

- Typography hierarchy:

- Title of website (Claire Daverley) will be bold sans serif font

- Navigation bar will be smaller, same font as title

- Page headers and the titles of her books will be only slightly smaller than the website title, same font 

- All body text will not be bold, and will be a similar sans serif font to the rest of the site (with both upper and lowercase letters)

- Embedded modules: podcasts, YouTube videos, digital articles, and social media features will be available as widgets within the website
