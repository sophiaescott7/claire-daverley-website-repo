# **PROJECT OVERVIEW**

## WHAT

This project is a redesign of Claire Daverley’s author website, whose next book is set to release in the US in 2027, following her debut title *Talking at Night*. In preparation for this exciting milestone, a website upgrade in aesthetic, ease of purchase, and inclusion of interactive opportunities will encourage reader engagement. 

## WHY

For readers of Claire Daverley, who want a deeper connection to the author and her work, the author website is a central hub for discovering her titles and following her creative presence online. This site is for literary and contemporary romance readers to provide connection opportunities with the author and access to her books by creating an immersive, visually cohesive brand experience.

## TARGET AUDIENCE

- **Primary**: Women age 18-35 who are fans of Claire Daverley’s books (*Talking at Night, People in Love*) 

- **Secondary**: Readers of comparative authors to Claire Daverley (Sally Rooney, Alice Winn, Gabrielle Zevin, etc.)

- **Tertiary**: Readers of literary fiction and contemporary romance books 

**Persona**: Meet Gemma, 24, a marketing associate living in an apartment with a few roommates in a large metropolitan area. She is an avid reader across genres, from literary fiction to romance. Gemma enjoys attending in-person events, especially book clubs and concerts.

## OBJECTIVES

- Increase in click-throughs to retailer sites to purchase either of Daverley’s titles: establish baseline upon launch, increase by 20% in the first 2 months

- Increase attendance at Daverley’s in-person events (RSVP rate): events sold out within 1 week of posting

- Grow Daverley’s following on her social media platforms by 20% in the first 3 months 

- Maximize user engagement with Daverley’s content (average session length): Target = 3 mins, Stretch = 5 minutes

- Develop a mobile-responsive design that maintains 90% desktop functionality

## COMPETITORS AND MARKET POSITION

Claire Daverley’s author website will differentiate itself from other author websites by:

- Displaying a more curated aesthetic tied to her book cover design 

- Providing more prominent purchase pathways for the author’s titles

- Integrating the author’s media appearances (podcasts, video interviews) for reader interaction

- Embedding the author’s social media platforms (Substack, Instagram) to encourage subscriptions / followings

# **DESIGN OVERVIEW**

## BRAND GUIDELINES

- **Color Palette: **Vivid pink (#FC3C71), pastel blue (#A6B8D9), orange (#FB481D), green/brown (#72630C)

- **Typography: **Sans-serif for headings (Montserrat Bold), Serif for body (Lora)

- **Imagery: **Art from the artists who created the cover art for both of Davreley’s titles

- **Tone of Voice: **Friendly yet factual, while mirroring the tone of the author’s writing

## INNOVATIVE UX CONSIDERATIONS

- **Character Quiz**: A quiz that tells readers which book character they are most like, along with a link to buy the book the character is from.

- **Time-Aware Background Art**: The cover of Daverley’s debut title, *Talking at Night*, features a sunset. As the day goes on, the background colors of the website could shift (in the art style of the cover) to suit the time of day.

- **Extras for Readers**: For readers who own copies of Daverley’s books, they can enter a code found on the back cover to unlock exclusive author interviews, bonus chapters, etc.

- **“Reader Postcards” Wall**: Fans can submit short notes about what the books meant to them via a portal on the website, then they can view a wall of digital postcards from fellow readers.

## TECHNICAL CONSTRAINTS

- Built on a Content Management System for flexibility and scalability 

- Fully accessible, meeting WCAG 2.1 AA standards 

- Optimized for fast loading, targeting a PageSpeed score of 90+

## SUCCESS METRICS (KPIs)

- **User Engagement: **Average session duration of 3+ minutes, 2+ pages per session

- **Community Growth: **15% month-over-month growth in Substack subscribers and/or Instagram followers

- **Content Effectiveness: **25% reduction in bounce rate for events page and product pages 

- **Total Utilization: **75% of users click on links to retailer sites monthly to purchase Daverley’s titles 

- **Revenue: **10% conversion rate from site visits to book purchases via retailers

## BUDGET

**Total Budget: **$0 (*SUBJECT TO CHANGE*)

## SCOPE AND DELIVERABLES

- Responsive website design (desktop, tablet, mobile)

- Content Management System for ease in updating content

- Embedded widgets for author interviews (podcasts, YouTube vides)

- Embedded links for the following:

- Events pages (retailer and venue sites)

- Author’s social media platforms (Instagram, Substack)

- Integration with retailer sites and e-commerce platforms for sale of Daverley’s titles
