# Style Guide — Claire Daverley Author Website

Compiled from `design-brief.md` and `requirements.md`.

## Colors
| Name | Hex |
|---|---|
| Vivid pink | `#FC3C71` |
| Pastel blue | `#A6B8D9` |
| Orange | `#FB481D` |
| Green/brown | `#72630C` |

## Typography
- Headings: Montserrat Bold (sans-serif)
- Body: Lora (serif) — per design brief
- Hierarchy (requirements doc): site title bold sans-serif; nav smaller, same font; page headers and book titles slightly smaller than site title; body text not bold, upper and lowercase.

## Imagery
- Art from the artists who created the cover art for both titles
- Background: colorful artwork reminiscent of the *Talking at Night* cover (sunset)

## Buttons
- White background, rectangular with rounded edges, black sans-serif text

## Navigation and footer
- Nav bar: one color from the background, white text for all items except "Books", which uses a bright background color to guide visitors to purchase
- Footer text: white, on every page

## Tone of voice
Friendly yet factual, mirroring the tone of the author's writing.

## Accessibility and performance
- WCAG 2.1 AA
- PageSpeed target 90+

## OPEN QUESTION (resolve before building)
The design brief specifies **Lora (serif) for body text**, but the requirements doc says **a sans-serif font throughout all pages**. Decide which applies.
