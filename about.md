# About page

Author Claire Daverley wrote ***[Talking at Night](talking-at-night.html)***, and more recently ***[People in Love](people-in-love.html)***. She also offers a variety of [freelance services](#freelancingservices), including copywriting, consulting, and digital marketing.

## **about the author: Claire Daverley**

Claire Daverley was born in 1991 and has been writing stories and creating compelling characters since her early years. After graduating from Oxford University with a degree in Fine Art, she began a career in publishing, working in digital marketing for [Penguin Random House](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/). Daverley promoted published titles by day while working on her own by night.

She specializes in crafting narratives at the intersection of contemporary romance and literary fiction. Her books follow childhood friends as they become lovers, distant companions as they rekindle their passion years later, and stories spanning decades. While her novels first debuted in the United Kingdom, she has gained a presence in the United States and continues to grow.

She released her debut novel ***[Talking at Night](talking-at-night.html)*** in 2023, and it is available in Europe, the UK, and the US. Her second novel, ***[People in Love](people-in-love.html)*** is out now in Europe and the UK, and will be available in the US in January 2027.

Originally from Hertfordshire, Claire now lives in Scotland by the sea with her husband and spaniel.
